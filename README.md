# 🛒 Online Retail Sales Analysis using SQL

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes an online retail business using MySQL to uncover sales trends, customer purchasing behavior, product performance, and business opportunities.

The project includes data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, business KPIs, customer analytics, and RFM segmentation.

## 📊 Dataset
- **Dataset:** UCI Online Retail Dataset
- **Source:** [UCI Machine Learning Repository](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/352/online+retail)
- **Original Records:** 541,909
- **Cleaned Records:** 536,641
- **Countries:** 38
- **Time Period:** December 2010 – December 2011

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- MySQL
- MySQL Workbench
- SQL

## 🔍 SQL Skills Demonstrated
- Data Cleaning and Duplicate Removal
- Aggregate Functions (`SUM`, `AVG`, `COUNT`)
- `GROUP BY` and `ORDER BY`

- RFM Analysis

## 📈 Analysis Performed
- Business KPI Analysis
- Monthly Revenue Trends
- Country-wise Sales Analysis
- Product Revenue and Quantity Analysis
- Customer Spending and Purchase Frequency
- Customer Retention Analysis
- Repeat Customer Analysis
- Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)
- Customer Acquisition Analysis
- Return and Cancellation Analysis
- RFM Customer Segmentation

## 💡 Key Business Insights
- The United Kingdom contributes the largest share of revenue.
- November 2011 recorded the highest monthly revenue.
- Approximately 70% of customers made repeat purchases.
- A small group of high-value customers contributes significantly to revenue.
- Customer segmentation helps identify champions, loyal customers, and customers at risk.

## 🎯 Business Recommendations
- Strengthen loyalty programs for repeat customers.
- Develop targeted marketing campaigns for high-value customers.
- Reactivate customers who have not purchased recently.
- Investigate product returns and cancellation patterns.
- Improve customer retention through personalized offers.
